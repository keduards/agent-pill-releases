## What it does

- **One ring per chat:** ChatGPT and Claude in Chrome, Arc, Brave or Edge, the Claude app (chats, Cowork and Code), and Claude Code in any terminal.
- **At a glance:** blue while a chat works, orange when it needs you (a permission or a question), green when it's done and you haven't looked yet.
- **One click away:** click a ring to jump straight to that chat; hover for the list of all of them. A notification, and a little bounce, when one finishes or needs you.
- **Private:** it reads window titles and buttons on your Mac, never your messages. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Install

1. **[Download AgentPill.dmg](https://github.com/keduards/agent-pill-releases/releases/latest/download/AgentPill.dmg)**, open it and drag **Agent Pill** into **Applications**.
2. Open Agent Pill from Applications.
3. **First time only:** macOS says it can't verify the app, because it isn't notarized by Apple yet. It's safe. Click **Done** (not Move to Trash), then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll all the way down, click **Open Anyway** and confirm with your password or Touch ID.

<p align="center">
  <img src="docs/open-anyway.webp" width="560" alt="System Settings: click Privacy &amp; Security, scroll down to Security, click Open Anyway">
</p>

4. The quick start takes about a minute: pick a theme and size, switch on Accessibility (it shows you where), and add the Chrome extension if you use ChatGPT or Claude in Chrome.

That's it. New versions install themselves when your chats are idle.
